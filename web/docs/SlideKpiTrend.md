23 · KPI + Trend. Three or four headline numbers and the trend of one of them.
Tiles have no ground — a rule on top divides them, and the plotted tile's rule is
cat-1 and thick, which is the only link between tile and chart. Every `delta`
names what it is compared with; `good` colours only the ▲/▼ glyph (ok or risk),
the words stay ink. `trend.target` is a goal or limit, a mute hairline named at
its end. `spark` adds a mute history beside the delta. With no trend worth
telling, use `SlideKeyFigures`. `source` and `takeaway` are required.
