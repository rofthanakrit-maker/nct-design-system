10 · Closing / Contact. The bookend to layout 01 — use once. The composition
follows `brand`.

`corp` (the default): teal→navy, the ask (`nextSteps`, `decisionBy`) and
`contact` down the left, the photo band on the right. `imageMode="full"` runs
the photograph edge to edge behind a scrim instead — React only, the .potx has
no such variant.

`web`: the web cover's split, mirrored. Flat navy carries the ask — title, the
numbered `nextSteps`, and `decisionBy` in its own boxed callout — and a paper
contact card stands on the right with `image` at its head (16:9;
`photoHandshake` is prepared at exactly that), `contactLabel` (default
"ติดต่อ"), `contact` and the lockup. `imageMode` is ignored. Keep `nextSteps`
to four one-line actions and `decisionBy` to two lines.
