24 · Before → After. A dumbbell per item — before in seq-300, after in cat-1 —
with the signed change on every row. Three to seven rows, all in one unit (mixed
units: convert to % of the old value, or split the slide); the layout sorts them
largest change first. `better` only sets the intro's "which way is good"; the
colours never change with it. `source` and `takeaway` are required, and the full
before/after table belongs in the appendix.
