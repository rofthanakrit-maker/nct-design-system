25 · Project Team. The project lead over two to four roles, joined by a
two-tier tree — a proposal names who it commits, not the client's org chart.
Each card reads `role` (with a head count, "นักพัฒนาระบบ × 3"), `name` (for a
role of several, the one who leads it), `credential` (one line, what the TOR
asks for) and two or three `duties`. More than four roles: merge them, and put
the roster and CVs in the appendix, said in `note`. The `.potx` layout always
has four role cards.
