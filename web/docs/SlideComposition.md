22 · Composition. Part-to-whole as one to three horizontal bars — never a pie.
One bar is a budget by category: the breakdown above it carries each value and
its share. Two or three compare packages: the breakdown is only the legend, each
bar carries its total at its end, and the full numbers go to the appendix. Up to
four segments; past four, keep the three largest and fold the rest into "อื่น ๆ"
with `other` (drawn cat-mute). Colour follows the segment, never its size.
`mode="amount"` scales bars to the largest total; `recommended` marks the
package the takeaway argues for. `source` and `takeaway` are required.
